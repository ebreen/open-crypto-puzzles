## 2. Reverse image search the `LifeFlashBeforeEyes.mp4` clip stills

This video sits on `pxsqo`, the page immediately before the locked `c2ozw` gate. The riddle
on that same page asks whether the flashing images are "your life flashing before your eyes
or hallucinations" and then "Is this the end?". Identifiable stills so far: a night Saturn V
on the pad (Apollo 17 is the only night Saturn V launch), a couple walking a fenced hillside
at sunset, a boat-wake sunset against a Cabo-like arch rock, and long slit-scan / warp-tunnel
passages. Glimmer lyric concatenations (`glimmerintheocean`, `gettingcloserandcloser`,
`ontheotherside`, `ifeelfreenow`) miss on `c2ozw`. If any still is identified as a named
film, music video, or band clip, that title is a strong East candidate. Costs on the order of
an hour with a reverse-image tool.
